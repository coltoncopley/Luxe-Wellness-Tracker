Overall assessment

From a product standpoint, I'd rate the current app 8.7/10. It already has a polished technical foundation and a clear niche. If you evolve it from a clinic companion into an AI-powered personal wellness operating system, I believe it has the potential to become a category-leading consumer health app rather than just one associated with a single med spa. The biggest shift is to make users feel that the app understands them every day, with Luxe naturally woven into that experience instead of sitting at the center of it.
