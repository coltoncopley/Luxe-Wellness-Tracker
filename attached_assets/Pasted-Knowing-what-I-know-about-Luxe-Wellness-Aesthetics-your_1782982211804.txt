Knowing what I know about Luxe Wellness & Aesthetics, your existing services, and the fact that you already use Aesthetic Record for scheduling, charting, memberships, and photos, I would not build another booking app.

The goal

Turn Luxe into the "health, beauty, and longevity" app instead of just the "appointment" app.

This approach transforms the app from something people use a few times a year into something they use every day. The daily engagement builds trust, generates referrals, and naturally funnels users into your core services—Botox, laser treatments, weight loss, IV therapy, BHRT, and skincare—without making the app feel like a constant advertisement.

I also think there's an opportunity to make Luxe AI the first nationally recognized AI wellness and aesthetics coach. With the right execution, that product could attract users far beyond your local market and become valuable even for people who never visit your physical clinic.
